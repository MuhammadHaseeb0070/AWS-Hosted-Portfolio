Popups, Forms, and UX Details
Modal Behavior
•	Modals open centered with a semi-opaque backdrop.
•	Live Transfers modal includes a short checklist of billing criteria and a configurable time buffer field for buyers.
•	Lead Generation modal includes sample targeting options and a checkbox for required compliance documentation.
•	All modals include a clear close button and keyboard escape support.
Form UX
•	Use client-side validation for required fields and email format.
•	Provide helpful inline hints for fields like Monthly Volume and Target Vertical.
•	Include a progress indicator for multi-step forms if needed.
•	After submission, show a confirmation screen with next steps and expected response time.
Form Submission
•	Route to business email and store a copy in a CRM or spreadsheet.
•	Include UTM capture fields for marketing attribution.

Design and Developer Notes
Visual Style
•	Palette: Blue/green primary for trust; orange accent for CTAs.
•	Typography: Professional sans-serif for headings and body.
•	Imagery: Trust-building icons, abstract illustrations, and professional team photo. Avoid location-specific imagery that could reduce trust.
Responsiveness
•	Mobile-first responsive design.
•	Sticky header with CTA visible on scroll.
•	Ensure modals and forms are fully usable on small screens.
Performance
•	Optimize images and use lazy loading.
•	Minimize third-party scripts to keep load times fast.
Accessibility
•	Ensure color contrast meets WCAG AA.
•	Provide keyboard navigation and ARIA labels for modals and forms.
Analytics and Tracking
•	Install analytics for page views, form submissions, and CTA clicks.
•	Track form conversions and UTM parameters.
Security and Compliance
•	Use HTTPS site-wide.
•	Ensure form data is transmitted securely and stored per applicable data protection standards.
•	Include a privacy policy link in the footer.
